MODIFICATION SURFACE OPTIONS (RESEARCH Pitfall 5 + Open Question 2):

Risk: env_loader.py override=True might NOT touch OPENAI_BASE_URL if the .env file doesn't carry that key by default. Inspection deliverable: confirm or refute.
